## Outcome

cortex's specification is validated and generated by ESS 0.59.0, locally and in CI, and the
`Check` workflow passes with the `ess` 0.59.0 binary.

## Why

`spec/ess-inputs.yaml` requires `ess 0.55.0`, and every `--strict-requires` step refuses a newer
binary. ESS 0.59.0 also refuses the specification as written: `ess specify validate --path spec`
reports one `ESS-COMMAND-004` (`conflicting_declaration`) on `cortex.instance.RunSource`: the
outcome `ran` is selected by the held state, which answers before the external branch
`fetch-failed` declared above it (measured 2026-10-13 with `ess` 0.59.0).

## Work

1. `spec/`: resolve `ESS-COMMAND-004` the way ESS's hint says (declaration order matches
   precedence), with no change in behaviour; `requires: ess 0.59.0`.
2. `task generate` with `ess` 0.59.0: `generated/`, `spec/suite.json` and the derived site files.
3. Make the crate build and pass against the regenerated code.
4. `.github/workflows/check.yml`: `ESS_VERSION` 0.59.0 and its `ESS_SHA256` from the release's
   `SHA256SUMS`.
5. `AGENTS.md`: the pin sentence and every upstream-facts row that cites an ESS version, checked
   against 0.59.0's output.

## Acceptance

- `ess specify validate --path spec --strict-requires` with 0.59.0 exits 0.
- `task drift` and `task docs-check` with 0.59.0 exit 0; `cargo test -p cortex-cli` passes,
  the conformance suite included.
- The `Check` workflow on the wave's pull request installs 0.59.0 and passes.

## Not in this story

ESS 0.60.0, which is still in its release pass; a later wave takes it.
