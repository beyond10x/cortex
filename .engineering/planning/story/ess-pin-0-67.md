## Outcome

cortex's specification is validated and generated by ESS 0.67.0, the newest release, locally and in
CI, and the `Check` workflow passes with the `ess` 0.67.0 binary.

## Why

The workspace rule is to use the newest ESS release everywhere. `spec/ess-inputs.yaml` requires
`ess 0.60.0`, and every `--strict-requires` step refuses a newer binary. cortex 1.0.0 is cut on the
newest ESS (`story:release-1-0`).

## Work

1. `spec/ess-inputs.yaml`: `requires: ess 0.67.0`; resolve whatever 0.67.0 refuses in `spec/`
   with no change in behaviour.
2. `task generate` with `ess` 0.67.0: `generated/`, `spec/suite.json` and the derived site files.
3. Make the crate build and pass against the regenerated code.
4. `.github/workflows/check.yml`: `ESS_VERSION` 0.67.0 and its `ESS_SHA256`
   (`7ae57d8d627227784c7a309a1a3d4cccda61b626cbab1749179af9f4f3451e8c` for
   `ess-0.67.0-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu.tar.gz`, from the release's `SHA256SUMS`).
5. `AGENTS.md`: the pin sentence and every upstream-facts row that cites an ESS version, checked
   against 0.67.0's output.

## Acceptance

- `ess specify validate --path spec --strict-requires` with 0.67.0 exits 0.
- `task check ESS=<ess 0.67.0>` exits 0, `drift` and `docs-check` included.
- The `Check` workflow on the wave's pull request installs 0.67.0 and passes.
