## Outcome

The PostgreSQL record store has no deadline on any call, and `Remote::call` holds the process
mutex while it writes to and reads from a child (`src/ask/records/postgres.rs:294-305`, `:516`,
`:543-555`), so a stalled database hangs every ask, vote and review until restart (R1-11).

## Acceptance

A stand-in records child that never answers: a record call fails within the configured deadline
with a `store-failed` outcome, and a second call afterwards is not blocked by the first (new
test).

## Findings it closes

R1-11.
