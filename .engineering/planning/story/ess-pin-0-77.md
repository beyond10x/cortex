## Outcome

cortex's specification is validated and generated by ESS 0.77.0, locally and in CI, and the
`Check` workflow passes with the `ess` 0.77.0 binary.

## Why

`spec/ess-inputs.yaml` requires `ess 0.60.0`; the machine's `ess` is 0.77.0, and the beyond10x
ESS rule moves a repository's pin in the wave that touches it anyway. Under 0.77.0 the
specification validates (4 files) and reports completeness: unscenarioed 14, unanswered 26
(measured 2026-10-30, wave 20261030a hardening).

## Work

1. `ess specify toolchain install 0.77.0 --pin` (`requires: ess 0.77.0`), validate with
   `--strict-requires`; resolve any new refusal the way its hint says, with no change in behaviour.
2. `task generate`: `generated/`, `spec/suite.json` and the derived site files; the
   `.ess-output` records move with them.
3. Make the crate build and pass against the regenerated code.
4. `.github/workflows/check.yml`: `ESS_VERSION` 0.77.0 and its `ESS_SHA256` from the release's
   `SHA256SUMS`.
5. `AGENTS.md`: the pin sentence (and its format, `ess/22`) and every upstream-facts row that
   cites an ESS version, checked against 0.77.0's output.
6. Two spec gaps the wave introduced (design review `review-result:design-review-w30a-spec-pass-1`):
   `SourceRan` carries `facts_reapplied`; `CastVote` and `CloseReview` declare the record routes'
   413 `bad-request` and 500 `store-failed` answers. An outcome that needs conformance target work
   beyond the spec moves to `story:spec-matches-design`.

## Acceptance

- `ess specify validate --path spec --strict-requires` with 0.77.0 exits 0.
- `task drift` and `task docs-check` with 0.77.0 exit 0; `cargo test -p cortex-cli --test
  conformance` passes, and its scenario floor is the regenerated suite's count.
- The `Check` workflow on the wave's pull request installs 0.77.0 and passes.
- `SourceRan` names `facts_reapplied`; `CastVote` and `CloseReview` name `bad-request` and
  `store-failed`, or `story:spec-matches-design` says why not.

## Not in this story

Raising the format ess/22 → ess/32 (`story:spec-format-ess-32`).
